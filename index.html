Block King

Mobile puzzle game by Novera Studio · Android · com.blockking.game

Purpose of the app

Block King is a block-placement puzzle game. Players place colorful blocks on an 8×8 board, clear lines, build combos, and chase high scores in Endless mode, daily challenges, campaign levels, and optional Google Play Games leaderboards.

  • Endless high-score runs
  • Daily & monthly challenges
  • 50-level campaign
  • Optional Google Play Games sign-in and cloud leaderboards
  • Rewarded ads to continue or get hints (AdMob)
